Market Demand from Willingness to Pay

Practice deriving the market demand schedule by horizontally summing individual demands.

At any price P, the market quantity demanded is the total count of units,across every consumer,whose willingness to pay is at least P:   Q(P) = Σᵢ #{ k : WTPᵢ,ₖ ≥ P }

Tip. Market demand is a step function. As the price falls past a WTP value, the quantity demanded jumps up — by one unit per entry sitting at that WTP. If two consumers happen to share the same WTP, the curve jumps by two units there and so on. To find Q(P), just count every entry in every consumer's table that is at least P.
