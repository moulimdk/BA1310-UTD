Different Types of Costs

Explicit Costs · Implicit Costs · Sunk Costs · Economic vs. Accounting Profit — practice problems

Explicit Costs are actual cash payments made by the firm or individual (out-of-pocket expenses). These appear in financial statements.

Implicit Costs are the opportunity costs of using self-owned resources (for example, foregone salary or interest). These do not involve a direct cash outflow.

Sunk Costs are costs that have already been incurred and cannot be recovered (or only partially recovered). They should be ignored in future decision-making.

Accounting Profit = Total Revenue − Explicit Costs

Economic Profit = Total Revenue − Explicit Costs − Implicit Costs

Economic profit accounts for opportunity costs and shows true resource allocation efficiency.
