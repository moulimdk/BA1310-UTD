Market Supply from Willingness to Sell

Practice deriving the market supply schedule by horizontally summing individual supplies.

At any price P, the market quantity supplied is the total count of units, across every seller, whose willingness to sell is at most P:   Q(P) = Σᵢ #{ k : WTSᵢ,ₖ ≤ P }

Tip. Market supply is a step function. As the price rises past a WTS value, the quantity supplied jumps up — by one unit per entry sitting at that WTS. If two sellers happen to share the same WTS, the curve jumps by two units there and so on. To find Q(P), just count every unit in every seller's list whose WTS is at most P.
